Daily Fitness Tips

💧

Stay Hydrated

Drink water before, during, and after your workouts to keep your muscles functioning at their peak and prevent early fatigue.

⏱️

Consistency is Key

A focused 20-minute daily workout is far more effective than a grueling 2-hour session once a week. Build a habit that lasts.

🛌

Prioritize Rest

Your muscles actually grow when you rest. Ensure you get 7-9 hours of sleep every night for optimal recovery.
