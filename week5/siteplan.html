  • What do I keep fit and stay healthy?

Color Schema:

  • Primary Color: Power Red (#D32F2F) which stands for energy, strength and motivation
  • Secondary Color: Deep Charcoal (#1C1C1C) which stands for Strength, seriousness and professionalism
  • Accent Color: Fitness Green (#2E7D32) which stands for health, progress and growth
  • Background Color: White (#FFFFFF), clean, breathable and modern

Typography: Roboto and san-serif
